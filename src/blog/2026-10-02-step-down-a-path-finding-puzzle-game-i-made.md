I've started making a game a lot of times, but I've finally finished one enough to release it. It's a simple path finding puzzle game that runs in a browser. The goal is to clear all numbered cells by moving over them to decrease the number in them and ending back in the middle when all cells are cleared.

It currently has 60 manually made levels in the campaign, a daily level and randomly generated ones with different difficulty settings. Once I add more levels to the campaign and it's been tested a bit more, I'll probably make an app out of it and might also put it on itch.io.

[You can play it in your browser here.](https://stepdown.rudyisme.com) It supports keyboard and touch (swiping). Have fun!
